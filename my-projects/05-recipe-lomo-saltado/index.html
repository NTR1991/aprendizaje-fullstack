Lomo Saltado

Ingredients

  • potato
  • onion
  • tomato
  • meat
  • cilantro
  • garlic
  • soy sauce
  • salt, pepper, MSG

Instructions

  1. sear the meat
  2. season with salt and pepper and add the garlic
  3. add and sauté the tomato with the onion
  4. add soy sauce, adjust seasoning, and add cilantro
